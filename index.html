Common Questions

New to Coaching? Here Is What You Need to Know.

Understand how professional coaching works, how it differs from therapy and consulting, and how it accelerates your career.

Is coaching only for people who are struggling or stuck? expand_more

Not at all. In fact, the world's most successful CEOs, tech founders, and elite performers partner with coaches precisely because they are high performers looking to reach their next peak. As Eric Schmidt (former CEO of Google) famously shared: "The best advice I ever got was to have a coach." Coaching is not remedial; it is a high-leverage partnership to accelerate your strategic impact, decision clarity, and executive presence.

What is professional coaching, and how does it work? expand_more

Professional coaching is a collaborative, forward-focused partnership designed to help you clarify your vision, break through blind spots, and achieve measurable career and leadership milestones. Through regular 1:1 sessions, strategic decision frameworks, and accountability, your coach helps you navigate high-stakes moments and operate at your highest potential.

How is coaching different from therapy, consulting, and mentoring? expand_more

While therapy focuses on healing past psychological trauma and consulting provides prescriptive technical solutions, coaching is forward-looking and performance-oriented. A coach does not tell you what to do like a consultant or analyze past trauma like a therapist; rather, a coach partners with you to develop self-mastery, strategic clarity, and actionable execution for your future.

Who benefits most from working with Gagan? expand_more

Gagan works with individuals across every stage of life and work, from recent graduates transitioning from campus to corporate, to individuals navigating personal life transitions, mindset blocks, or mid-life reinvention, as well as ambitious professionals, immigrant talent, and senior leaders looking to accelerate their career impact.

Is our coaching engagement completely confidential? expand_more

Yes, 100%. As an ICF Certified Coach, Gagan adheres strictly to the International Coaching Federation Code of Ethics. Everything discussed during your sessions remains strictly confidential and is never disclosed to employers or third parties.

How do I get started? expand_more

The first step is a complimentary 30-minute Discovery Call. We discuss your current career inflection point, clarify what success looks like, and determine if coaching together is the right mutual fit.
