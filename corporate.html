Corporate Advisory FAQs

Questions from Executive Sponsors & People Leaders

Answers to common questions regarding corporate engagement setup, confidentiality, and alignment.

How are corporate coaching engagements initiated and structured? expand_more

Corporate engagements typically begin with an exploratory consultation with the sponsor (HR executive, department head, or CEO) to define organizational outcomes and talent objectives. We then conduct a three-way alignment conversation with the coachee before launching 6-month or 12-month retainers.

How do you protect client confidentiality in sponsored engagements? expand_more

Strict confidentiality is the bedrock of coaching efficacy. As an ICF Certified Coach, Gagan strictly adheres to the ICF Code of Ethics. While overarching development goals and progress milestones are aligned with the executive sponsor, specific session dialogue remains 100% confidential between Gagan and the leader.

Can programs be customized for immigrant or diverse talent cohorts? expand_more

Yes. Gagan specializes in decoding unwritten Western corporate cultural dynamics, boardroom presence, and executive voice for high-potential immigrant and technical contributors who have exceptional technical competence but need support bridging into senior executive leadership.

How do you measure engagement success and enterprise ROI? expand_more

We establish qualitative and quantitative key indicators at the start of each retainer, including 360 stakeholder feedback, delivery acceleration, cross-functional relationship health, and leadership promotion milestones.
